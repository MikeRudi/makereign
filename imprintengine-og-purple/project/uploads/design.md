\# Design System (DHK × Apple Hybrid)



\## Philosophy

\- Quiet confidence

\- Minimal, intentional, controlled

\- Design-led, not content-led

\- Precision over decoration

\- Every element must justify its presence



\---



\## Core Principles



\- One idea per section

\- Reduce everything to its essential form

\- Clarity over cleverness

\- Visual hierarchy is more important than content volume

\- Restraint is a feature, not a limitation



\---



\## Layout System



\- Full-width sections

\- Strong vertical flow

\- No clutter or multi-column noise unless necessary

\- Content is centered within a controlled max width



\### Container

\- Max width: 1200px

\- Wide sections (imagery): full-bleed



\---



\## Spacing System



Use a strict spacing scale:



\- xs: 8px

\- sm: 16px

\- md: 24px

\- lg: 48px

\- xl: 80px

\- xxl: 120px



Rules:

\- Minimum 80px between sections

\- Large sections use 120px+

\- Generous padding around all elements

\- Never allow cramped layouts



\---



\## Typography



\### Style

\- Bold, confident, minimal

\- Short line lengths

\- No dense paragraphs



\### Scale

\- H1: 64–80px / bold / tight line height

\- H2: 40–48px / medium

\- H3: 24–28px / medium

\- Body: 16–18px / regular



\### Rules

\- Maximum 1–2 lines per paragraph

\- Maximum 6–10 words per heading

\- Prefer statements over explanations

\- Repetition is allowed if it reinforces clarity



\---



\## Color



\- Primarily monochrome (black / white / neutral)

\- One accent color only (used sparingly)

\- No heavy gradients unless extremely subtle

\- Avoid visual noise



\---



\## Imagery



\- Large, dominant visuals

\- Image-first, text-second

\- Clean, architectural, premium feel

\- Use real-world, tactile, or production visuals where possible

\- Avoid generic stock imagery



\---



\## Components



\### Hero



\- One strong statement (H1)

\- Optional supporting line (1 line only)

\- Full-bleed image or video

\- No clutter

\- No multiple CTAs



\---



\### Section



Each section must contain only ONE idea:



Allowed structure:

\- Heading

\- Optional single-line description

\- One visual (image or video)



\---



\### Services Block



\- Segmented or stacked layout

\- Each service = short label + minimal description

\- No long explanations

\- Expand or navigate for deeper info



\---



\### Case Study / Proof Block



\- Large visual

\- Minimal text:

&#x20; - Title

&#x20; - 1-line context

\- No dense storytelling



\---



\### Logos / Trust



\- Clean grid

\- Muted styling

\- No visual competition



\---



\## Interaction \& Motion



\- Smooth scrolling

\- Slow, intentional transitions

\- Subtle fades and movement only

\- No aggressive animation

\- No parallax overload

\- Motion must never distract from content



\---



\## Content Rules



\- Reduce everything to essentials

\- No long-form explanations

\- No marketing fluff

\- Avoid buzzwords where possible

\- Show > tell



\---



\## UI Restraint (Apple Influence)



\- Remove anything unnecessary

\- Align everything precisely

\- Consistent spacing at all levels

\- Avoid over-styling components

\- Let typography and spacing carry the design



\---



\## What to Avoid



\- Dense UI blocks

\- Dashboard-style layouts

\- Overuse of cards or boxes

\- Bright, distracting colors

\- Heavy animations

\- Multi-message sections



\---



\## Overall Feel



The site should feel like:

\- A premium design studio

\- An architectural editorial layout

\- A system with intent and control



Not like:

\- A SaaS dashboard

\- An e-commerce store

\- A marketing-heavy corporate site
